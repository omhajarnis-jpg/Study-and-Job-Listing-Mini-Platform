Farm Field Assistant

Location: Bhavnagar District, Gujarat

Salary: ₹10,000 – ₹12,000 per month

Category: Agriculture / Manual Work

Job Description

We are seeking dedicated individuals to assist with day-to-day farm operations such as crop planting, irrigation, harvesting, and field maintenance. Candidates must be hardworking, reliable, and able to work in outdoor environments.

Requirements

  • Basic knowledge of crop handling or farm work.
  • Physically fit and willing to work in field conditions.
  • Minimum 10th standard education (preferred).
  • Experience not required but preferred.

Expectations

Workers are expected to maintain punctuality, assist with teamwork, and follow farm safety guidelines.
