Farm Assistant

📍 Green Valley, Madhya Pradesh

Job Description

We are looking for a dedicated Farm Assistant to help with crop management, irrigation, harvesting, and maintenance. No prior experience needed — training will be provided. A great opportunity for individuals looking to start a career in agriculture.

Responsibilities:

  • Assist with planting and watering crops
  • Operate simple farm equipment (training provided)
  • Maintain cleanliness around the farm
  • Support in packaging and transporting produce

Requirements:

  • Physically fit and able to work outdoors
  • Team player with a positive attitude
  • Basic literacy preferred

Salary:

₹8,000 - ₹10,000 per month (depending on experience)
